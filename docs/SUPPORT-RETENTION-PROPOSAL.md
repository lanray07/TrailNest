# Proposed support correspondence process

Status: owner approved on 2 November 2026: "Approve the 90-day process". Public policy updated; operation remains the owner’s monthly manual responsibility.

Approved default: retain an open support conversation while needed to resolve it, then delete or anonymise it within 90 days after closure. O. Bankole reviews closed TrailNest support threads monthly. Keep a minimal exception record only where a specific unresolved dispute or legal obligation requires it; record purpose and next review date. Do not retain raw purchase receipts, account identifiers or credentials in Git or troubleshooting reports. Handle deletion requests at the published support address and explain any justified exception. This process is manual; no automated deletion or mailbox access has been implemented or claimed.

Why 90 days: allows a short follow-up window for recurring technical issues without indefinite retention. It is an operator-selected proposal, not a statutory UK retention period. The operator approved the process and remains responsible for carrying it out. No existing mailbox contents were inspected, changed or deleted.

Reference checked 2 November 2026: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/ . ICO says retention should be justified by purpose and periodically reviewed; it does not prescribe a universal period. No legal-compliance certification is claimed.
