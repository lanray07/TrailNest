TrailNest

How can we help?

Email banksmi@mail.com with your app version, device model and a description of the problem. Do not include passwords, full receipts or private walk coordinates.

Your data

Journal entries and imports stay on your device. Delete them using the app's controls. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes private app data; backups are disabled. Save anything you need first.

Location and voice

Recording depends on location permission, device GPS and battery settings. It is not a safety tracker. Voice input depends on an installed compatible speech service; touch controls remain available.

Maps and routes in the 0.8.7 candidate

Optional maps load from OpenFreeMap. A completed download is needed for an offline basemap; a partial download is not ready. Downloading is limited to the foreground and a conservative data budget. The initial catalogue has two one-way Tamsin Trail sections with AI desk-review disclosures; they have not been field checked. Follow current signs and park notices, use daylight hours and plan your return. A translated interface does not imply worldwide reviewed routes.

Purchases

Purchasing is disabled in production candidate 0.8.6. The paid 0.8.7 candidate is saved in the Amazon submission draft and has not been released. Its verification service is configured; subscription availability depends on Amazon returning an eligible offer. When available, Amazon manages subscriptions and cancellation; uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.
