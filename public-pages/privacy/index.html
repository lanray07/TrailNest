TrailNest

Your walks are yours.

Privacy policy for TrailNest Android 0.8.6 and the 0.8.7 candidate. Updated 2 November 2026. Version 0.8.7 is a signed paid candidate in the Amazon submission draft, not a released app. Its production verifier is configured; store availability and release acceptance remain pending.

Who operates TrailNest

TrailNest is operated by O. Bankole, United Kingdom. For privacy questions, data requests or support, email banksmi@mail.com. Please do not send passwords, payment details or full purchase receipts.

Your private journal

Journal entries, notes, imported GPX routes, preferences and recording drafts are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Attached photos are copied into private app files and location metadata is removed from those copies. Original photos outside TrailNest are unchanged. TrailNest has no account, cloud sync, analytics or advertisements in this version. It does not upload your journal, photos or routes to a TrailNest service.

Location and walk recording

Location access is optional. When you explicitly start a recording, the app uses location coordinates in memory to estimate distance, then discards those coordinates. It saves a session identifier, start date, active duration, estimated distance and recovery state. It does not save a recorded coordinate track. Imported GPX geometry is stored separately on your device.

A location foreground service can continue recording with the screen off or the app minimised, subject to device and battery restrictions. It has an ongoing notification; visibility depends on your settings. Pause stops location updates and active time. After a process stop or device restart, a recovered draft stays paused until you choose to resume. Foreground route-progress location stops when the activity leaves the foreground. You can stop recording or revoke location permission in device settings.

Optional voice services and sharing

If you choose voice input, your installed Android speech recognition service may process audio on its provider's servers under that provider's privacy terms. TrailNest does not operate that service or retain an audio recording. Text returned by the service is handled as your chosen input. Touch input remains available. Spoken feedback uses the installed text-to-speech service and its settings.

Sharing or exporting sends the selected information to the app or destination you choose through Android's sharing controls. This happens after your action and is not live tracking. The recipient's own privacy practices apply.

Optional maps in the 0.8.7 candidate

Maps use MapLibre Native and load from OpenFreeMap after you choose Load map. Requests disclose your IP address, app-identifying User-Agent and requested map areas/assets to OpenFreeMap and its CDN provider Cloudflare. A viewed map area can reveal an approximate place of interest. Your journal, photos and notes are not included in these requests. Your position overlay is computed locally. Moving or viewing a map can request the corresponding map area.

Downloaded regions and their styles, sprites, glyphs and tiles are kept in the app's private map database. Deleting a saved region removes its offline association; shared or ordinary browsing cache can remain. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes private app storage. You can avoid external map requests by not loading maps. Location permission is separate and optional.

Provider information: OpenFreeMap privacy, OpenFreeMap terms and Cloudflare privacy.

Purchases

Purchasing is disabled in the 0.8.6 production candidate. It does not send Amazon account or receipt identifiers to TrailNest's receipt-verification service. Separate Amazon Live App Testing builds have different purchase-verification behaviour and are outside the scope of this production policy.

Receipt verification when production purchasing is enabled

The paid 0.8.7 candidate uses Amazon checkout. Amazon handles payment, renewal and cancellation; TrailNest does not collect card details. When checking or restoring an entitlement, the Amazon SDK supplies an Amazon user identifier and receipt identifier. TrailNest sends these over HTTPS to its Cloudflare-hosted verifier, which sends them to Amazon's Receipt Verification Service to check the annual product and current subscription status. This candidate has not yet been released; an offer is available only when Amazon supplies an eligible subscription product.

The verifier returns an expiring signed lease bound to hashed account/receipt identifiers, package and product. Billing state and the lease are stored privately on the device to support bounded offline access and restore. The verifier has no receipt database and does not intentionally log identifiers, request bodies, secrets or RVS URLs; application observability is disabled. Network metadata is used for rate limiting. Cloudflare and Amazon may process infrastructure/security metadata under their own policies. This does not mean those providers retain no logs.

Removing app data does not cancel an Amazon subscription. Manage or cancel it through Amazon. Restore while online to refresh entitlement. The maximum six-hour application lease is separate from the subscription period. Expired or revoked receipts cannot grant new access.

Keeping and deleting information

Local journal entries, copied photos and imported routes remain until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall TrailNest. Use the app's controls to delete walks and imports and to save or discard a recording draft. Deleting a copied photo in TrailNest does not delete an original photo elsewhere. Android backups are disabled. Clearing app data and uninstalling remove its private data and cannot be undone. Export imported GPX routes you want to keep; recorded walks do not contain exportable tracks.

If you email support, your message and email address are processed through email providers to respond to your request. We keep an open conversation while needed to resolve it, then delete or anonymise it within 90 days after closure. O. Bankole reviews closed TrailNest support conversations monthly. A specific unresolved dispute or legal obligation may require longer retention; we record the reason and review it again. Contact the address above to request deletion. This is a manual process, not an automatic email-deletion service. Provider-held records may require a separate request to that provider.

Contact O. Bankole at banksmi@mail.com to ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a portable copy where those rights apply. We may need proportionate information to verify a request and will explain any applicable exception. Please do not send passwords or full purchase receipts.
