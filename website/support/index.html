TrailNest

A little help for the trail.

Where are the walking routes?

Our UK catalogue is in preparation. No reviewed routes are currently published. Other countries are planned. A translated interface never implies local coverage.

Can I use my own GPX?

Import one continuous track or route, up to 5 MB and 50,000 points. Multiple track segments are rejected because joining them could hide gaps. Imports stay private and are labelled unreviewed. Importing a route does not establish rights of access.

What works offline?

Android stores route geometry locally and offers foreground location progress if the device provides a location fix. There are no basemap tiles, elevation data or validated turn instructions in this preview. The website stores geometry locally but needs a connection to load; browser data may be cleared by the device. One route can be marked as a free pack.

Why does location look uncertain?

Some Fire tablets lack GPS hardware. Indoor reception, weather and device capability can affect location. The app flags poor accuracy and rejects stale fixes. Carry an appropriate map and check your surroundings.

Purchases and support

Purchases are not configured in this build. A public support contact and operator identity must be supplied before the commercial release. For development issues, record the app version, device model and steps to reproduce in the project’s issue tracker.

Responsible access

Follow local signs and current land manager guidance. For England and Wales, consult the Countryside Code. Other countries and UK nations have their own access guidance.
