A little help for the trail.

Contact support

Email banksmi@mail.com with your app version, device model, what you expected and steps to reproduce the problem. Remove personal details from screenshots. Never send passwords, card details, account identifiers or full purchase receipts.

How do I record a walk?

On Android, open My walks → Start live recording and allow location access. Allow notifications to see recording controls outside the app. Recording can continue with the screen off or the app minimised; keep location enabled. Pause stops active time and location updates. Resume starts a new distance segment. Finish and review pauses recording; enter a title and choose Save walk to add it to your journal. Discard recording removes the draft after confirmation.

Battery restrictions and device location capability can interrupt recording. Some Fire tablets have no GPS, and inaccurate or stale fixes do not count towards distance. Active time may continue while waiting for an accurate fix, so a zero-distance entry is possible. This is a private journal tool, not emergency monitoring or validated navigation. The website does not record background walks.

What happens after an interruption?

Reopen My walks. A stored recording draft returns paused after process death, force-stop or a device restart; it never silently resumes. You can resume, finish and save, or discard it. Some measurements after the last checkpoint can be missing. Unfinished title/notes may be lost after process death, so re-enter them before saving. Do not clear app data or uninstall while you need a draft.

Where are the walking routes?

No reviewed routes are currently published. The UK catalogue is in preparation and other countries are planned. A translated interface never implies local route coverage.

Can I use my own GPX?

Import one continuous track or route, up to 5 MB and 50,000 points. Multiple track segments are rejected because joining them could hide gaps. Imports stay private and are labelled unreviewed. Importing a route does not establish rights of access. Recorded walks save aggregate measurements, not an exportable coordinate track.

What works offline?

Android stores imported geometry, the checklist and journal locally. Foreground route progress and background recording depend on usable device location. There are no licensed basemap tiles, elevation data or validated turn instructions. The website stores geometry locally but needs a connection to load; browser data may be cleared by the device. One imported route can be marked as a free pack.

Amazon test purchases

Amazon subscription integration is configured for Live App Testing, but purchase acceptance is not complete. The annual offer may be unavailable; Subscribe stays disabled until Amazon returns an eligible offer and the verifier is ready. Restore purchases refreshes the account, offer and receipt checks. If the offer is still missing, contact support with the app version and marketplace; repeated tapping cannot create an offer.

Premium currently permits additional private GPX geometry packs; it does not unlock licensed maps or curated routes. Normal release builds keep purchases disabled until configured and verified. Amazon manages checkout, subscriptions and cancellation. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. See Privacy for the account/receipt identifiers used during verification.

Responsible access

Follow local signs and current land manager guidance. For England and Wales, consult the Countryside Code. Other countries and UK nations have their own access guidance. Carry an appropriate map and check your surroundings.
