Privacy information for TrailNest Android development/LAT builds and the separate 0.1 web preview. Updated 24 October 2026.

Contact

For privacy questions or help with your data, email TrailNest at banksmi@mail.com. Do not send passwords, payment details or full purchase receipts.

What stays on your device

The website stores checklist choices, preferences, journal entries and imported GPX geometry in this browser’s local storage. Android stores journal entries, imported routes and photo references in a private database, with preferences and recording checkpoints in private app storage. Photos you attach are copied into private app files. Browser and Android data are separate and do not sync. There is no TrailNest login, analytics or advertising in these builds.

Location and background walk recording

The website does not request location or record background walks. On Android, you start recording explicitly from My walks and grant location access. A location foreground service can keep recording with the screen off or the app minimised, subject to device and battery restrictions. It has an ongoing notification; notification visibility depends on your settings. Pause stops location subscriptions and active time; resume starts a new distance segment. Finish pauses recording before review and save.

Recording uses coordinates in memory to estimate distance, then discards them. It stores only a session identifier, start date, active duration, estimated distance and recovery state. It does not save a recorded coordinate track or upload your walk to TrailNest servers. Imported GPX geometry is different: it remains stored locally until you delete the import. A saved journal entry includes your title, notes and aggregate measurements.

If the app process is stopped or the device restarts, a stored draft returns paused. Resume requires your action; recording never restarts automatically. Measurements since the last durable checkpoint may be lost. Unsaved review title/notes are not included in that checkpoint. Foreground route-progress location stops when the activity leaves the foreground.

Amazon purchases and receipt verification

Purchases are enabled only in configured Amazon test builds while live billing acceptance is pending. Amazon handles checkout and payment information. When checking or restoring a purchase, Android sends an Amazon account identifier and receipt identifier over HTTPS to TrailNest’s receipt-verification service hosted on Vercel. The service sends those identifiers to Amazon’s Receipt Verification Service to check the subscription, then returns a signed, short-lived entitlement. It does not receive your journal, coordinates, imported routes or card details.

The app keeps the signed entitlement privately on the device. The verifier has no persistent receipt database and does not deliberately log raw receipt/account identifiers. Request processing and rate limiting use network information such as IP addresses; Vercel and Amazon may process service/security logs under their own policies. A fixed hosting-log retention period has not yet been established for TrailNest. This must be resolved before commercial release; this page does not promise immediate deletion from provider logs.

Voice, sharing and other providers

Android’s installed speech recognition service may send audio to its provider; check that service’s settings and terms. Sharing sends selected information to the service you choose only after your action; it is not live tracking. Website hosting processes ordinary HTTP request information. Emailing support sends your message and email address through email providers; include only what is needed to resolve your question.

Keeping and deleting your data

Local entries and imports remain until you delete them or clear the relevant app/browser data. Delete walks, attached photo copies and imports using the app’s controls; removing an attached copy does not delete an original photo outside TrailNest. Save or discard a recording draft in My walks. Clearing website storage removes that browser’s preview data. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes its private data; Android backups are disabled in this build. These actions cannot be undone. Export imported GPX routes you want to keep; recorded walks do not contain exportable tracks.

Deleting TrailNest data or uninstalling does not cancel an Amazon subscription or erase Amazon’s purchase records. Manage subscriptions through Amazon. For privacy requests involving support correspondence or TrailNest-hosted processing, use the contact above; provider-held records may require a separate request to that provider.

Development status

No licensed basemaps or reviewed route catalogue are available. Operator legal details and provider retention arrangements still need to be completed before commercial release. This page describes implemented development features and does not represent completed legal approval.
