Your walks are yours.

Privacy information for the TrailNest 0.1 development preview. Updated 23 October 2026.

What stays on your device

The website stores checklist choices, preferences, walk journal entries and imported GPX geometry in this browser’s local storage. The Android app stores equivalent information in a private Room database and DataStore. No account, analytics, advertising or cloud sync is implemented.

Location and voice

The website does not request your location. The Android app requests location only when you choose it in a route view. Updates stop when you leave the foreground. No location history is sent to TrailNest servers. Android’s installed speech recognition service may send audio to its provider; check that provider’s privacy terms before using it.

Your choices

Delete journal entries and imports inside the app. Reset the checklist or clear this website’s browser storage to remove local data. Uninstalling the Android app clears its local data; Android backups are disabled in this build. Clearing data cannot be undone. Export GPX routes you want to keep.

Sharing and hosting

Sharing happens only after an explicit action and sends the selected plan through your chosen service. It provides no live tracking. A website hosting provider may process standard HTTP request information; its practices depend on the deployment selected.

Before a public product release

The operator’s legal identity, privacy contact, hosting retention policy and any future service providers must be recorded before publication as a commercial application. This development document does not claim legal review.
